Aura: replace the built-in ephemeris with Astronomy Engine 2.1.19

Aura's birth chart and moon pages used hand-rolled orbital element tables.
They drifted badly for the outer planets - Saturn was out by up to 0.71
degrees, enough to put it in the wrong sign on some dates - and the moon
pages stepped forward from a fixed new-moon epoch by the average synodic
month, which wanders by about a day.

Both now come from Astronomy Engine (MIT, Don Cross), bundled locally as
plain JavaScript so the planner still runs entirely offline from a file.

Positions
- Geocentric apparent ecliptic longitude via GeoVector + Ecliptic, which
  handles light-time, aberration, nutation and the true equinox of date.
- Mean error against PyMeeus over 1700-2200 drops to 0.003-0.007 degrees
  for the planets and 0.069 for the Moon. Saturn improves 216-fold.
- Pluto joins the chart, with generational wording in the readings.
- Placidus houses are unchanged at normal latitudes.

Moon
- Phase, age and illuminated fraction are measured, not extrapolated.
- Next new, full and quarter moons come from a real search, so the dates
  on Rituals & Moon are now accurate to the minute rather than the day.

Honesty guards, surfaced in the page's own notes rather than hidden
- Births outside 1700-2200 are flagged as outside the checked range.
- Inside the polar circles, where Placidus has no solution, the chart
  falls back to equal houses and says so; the true MC is kept separate.
- Birth times that never existed (spring forward) or happened twice
  (autumn) are flagged instead of being silently resolved.

Nothing else about the app changes: saved charts, exports and backups
read and write exactly as before. Lunar nodes, Chiron and asteroids are
deliberately not included here.
