SLOW INK LIFE - START HERE
===========================
A soft, all-in-one life planner with a notebook of its own.
Thank you for your purchase!

HOW TO OPEN IT
1. Unzip (extract) the folder first. Please don't open the planner from inside the zip file.
2. Put the unzipped folder somewhere permanent, such as your Documents folder.
3. Double-click the file named  index.html  - it opens in your web browser
   (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari all work).

That's all. There is nothing to install, no account to make and no internet
connection needed. Bookmark the page in your browser so it's easy to find.

KEEP YOUR WORK SAFE  (please read - it's important)
- Your planner saves automatically, inside your web browser on this device.
  Nothing is sent anywhere and nobody else can see it.
- Always open it in the SAME browser, from the SAME folder. Don't move, rename
  or re-download the folder once you've started, or the planner may open empty.
- Clearing your browser's "site data" or "cookies and site data" can erase the
  planner. Make a backup before you clean your browser.
- BACK UP REGULARLY:  open  Settings  (bottom of the left menu, or More > Settings on a small screen) and click "Export JSON".
  It downloads one small file. Keep it somewhere safe (cloud drive, email to
  yourself). To restore: open Settings and click "Import JSON", then choose your file.
- Moving to a new computer or browser? Make a backup, open the planner there,
  and restore the backup.
- Best on a laptop or desktop. Opening local files on phones and tablets varies
  by device and browser - if you'd like to use it on a tablet, get in touch.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- Yearly, Monthly, Weekly and Daily spreads, each with a matching Review page.
- Fitness, Meals & Recipes (recipe cards in sections like Breakfast and Drinks, with cover photos and photos on each recipe), Finance, Mind & Ikigai, Travel (itinerary, packing, outfit photos, budget and a photo album for each trip), Home & Chores.
- PRODUCTIVITY: Habits, Mood Log, Goals (plan, action steps, target date, progress
  and a review of how the plan is going), Projects (a planning framework, phases and steps
  with progress and a review), Vision Board and Mind Map (keep as many boards
  and maps as you like).
- NOTEBOOK (left menu > Journal > Notebook): a separate bound book with coloured
  section tabs (Journal, Notes, Ideas - add your own with the + tab). Seven
  papers: blank, lined, squared, dot grid, Cornell, and two and three columns.
  Switch between Type and Markup to handwrite with a
  pen, marker or pencil.
- Four colour themes, light / dark / auto, and three fonts (Settings, or the moon/sun button for quick dark mode).
- A Today dashboard with a getting-started checklist. Use "Look around with sample data" to explore, then "Start fresh" when you're ready.
