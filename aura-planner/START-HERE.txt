AURA - START HERE
==================
A glassy pastel life planner with a notebook of its own.
Thank you for your purchase!

HOW TO OPEN IT
1. Unzip (extract) the folder first. Please don't open the planner from inside the zip file.
2. Put the unzipped folder somewhere permanent, such as your Documents folder.
3. Double-click the file named  index.html  - it opens in your web browser
   (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari all work).

That's all. There is nothing to install, no account to make and no internet
connection needed. Bookmark the page in your browser so it's easy to find.

KEEP YOUR WORK SAFE  (please read - it's important)
- Your planner saves automatically, inside your web browser on this device.
  Nothing is sent anywhere and nobody else can see it.
- Always open it in the SAME browser, from the SAME folder. Don't move, rename
  or re-download the folder once you've started, or the planner may open empty.
- Clearing your browser's "site data" or "cookies and site data" can erase the
  planner. Make a backup before you clean your browser.
- BACK UP REGULARLY:  click the gear icon (top right), then "Export backup (.json)".
  It downloads one small file. Keep it somewhere safe (cloud drive, email to
  yourself). To restore: click the gear icon, then "Import backup", and choose your file.
- Moving to a new computer or browser? Make a backup, open the planner there,
  and restore the backup.
- Best on a laptop or desktop. Opening local files on phones and tablets varies
  by device and browser - if you'd like to use it on a tablet, get in touch.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- Year, Month, Week and Daily Focus pages.
- Life Design tools: Level 10 Life wheel, Ikigai, Eisenhower matrix, SMART goals,
  mind map and vision board.
- Wellness: Habits, Finances, Meals & Recipes (with photos) and Fitness.
- NOTEBOOK (top bar): a separate bound book with pastel section tabs (Journal,
  Notes, Ideas - add your own with the + tab), seven papers including Cornell and
  columns, and a Type / Markup switch for handwriting and sketching.
- AI COACH (optional): the habit and journal analyzer and priority synthesizer
  work on your device with no setup. If you want written coaching from Claude,
  you can add your OWN Anthropic API key under AI Coach > Coach settings. This
  is the only feature that uses the internet, it is completely optional, and the
  key is stored only in your browser.
