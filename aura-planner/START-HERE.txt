AURA - START HERE
==================
A glassy pastel manifestation planner with a notebook of its own.
Thank you for your purchase!

HOW TO OPEN IT
1. Unzip (extract) the folder first. Please don't open the planner from inside the zip file.
2. Put the unzipped folder somewhere permanent, such as your Documents folder.
3. Double-click the file named  index.html  - it opens in your web browser
   (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari all work).

That's all. There is nothing to install, no account to make and no internet
connection needed. Bookmark the page in your browser so it's easy to find.

KEEP YOUR WORK SAFE  (please read - it's important)
- Your planner saves automatically, inside your web browser on this device.
  Nothing is sent anywhere and nobody else can see it.
- Always open it in the SAME browser, from the SAME folder. Don't move, rename
  or re-download the folder once you've started, or the planner may open empty.
- Clearing your browser's "site data" or "cookies and site data" can erase the
  planner. Make a backup before you clean your browser.
- BACK UP REGULARLY:  click the gear icon (top right), then "Export backup (.json)".
  It downloads one small file. Keep it somewhere safe (cloud drive, email to
  yourself). To restore: click the gear icon, then "Import backup", and choose your file.
- Moving to a new computer or browser? Make a backup, open the planner there,
  and restore the backup.
- Best on a laptop or desktop. Opening local files on phones and tablets varies
  by device and browser - if you'd like to use it on a tablet, get in touch.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- HOME: your day at a glance, with a short getting-started checklist. Click
  "Look around with sample data" to see the planner filled in, then "Start fresh".
- PERSONALISE (the palette button, top right): your name for the greeting, four colour themes, light / dark /
  auto, and three fonts. The moon / sun button switches dark mode.
- PLANNER: Year, Month, Week and a Daily Practice page (intention, moon of the
  day, affirmation of the day, practice checklist, gratitude, feelings, reflection).
- MANIFEST: My Manifestations (a board with a progress check-in and a log of signs
  and wins), Scripting, guided Visualize sessions, Affirmations, and Rituals &
  Moon (369 method, 55 x 5, gratitude, signs, moon phases).
- ALIGN: Level 10 Life wheel, Ikigai, Dream Life Script (write your dream life area by area, then save the affirmations you like), Dream Map, Vision Board and Habits.
- NOTEBOOK (top bar): a separate bound book with section tabs, seven papers,
  headings, lists and links, photos, stickers, and a Type / Markup switch for
  handwriting and sketching.
- INSIGHTS: pattern insights and a thought sorter that work on your device with
  no setup. If you want written reflections from Claude you can add your OWN
  Anthropic API key under Insights > Pattern Insights. This is the only feature
  that uses the internet, it is completely optional, and the key is stored only
  in your browser.

A NOTE ON MANIFESTING
Aura is a tool for reflection, intention-setting and planning. It can't promise
any outcome. Use it to get clear about what you want, notice how you feel, and
take small steps.
