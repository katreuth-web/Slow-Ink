SLOW INK SAGE - START HERE
===========================
A warm, paper-and-sage yearly planner with a notebook of its own.
Thank you for your purchase!

HOW TO OPEN IT
1. Unzip (extract) the folder first. Please don't open the planner from inside the zip file.
2. Put the unzipped folder somewhere permanent, such as your Documents folder.
3. Double-click the file named  index.html  - it opens in your web browser
   (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari all work).

That's all. There is nothing to install, no account to make and no internet
connection needed. Bookmark the page in your browser so it's easy to find.

KEEP YOUR WORK SAFE  (please read - it's important)
- Your planner saves automatically, inside your web browser on this device.
  Nothing is sent anywhere and nobody else can see it.
- Always open it in the SAME browser, from the SAME folder. Don't move, rename
  or re-download the folder once you've started, or the planner may open empty.
- Clearing your browser's "site data" or "cookies and site data" can erase the
  planner. Make a backup before you clean your browser.
- BACK UP REGULARLY:  scroll to the very bottom of any page and click "Back up planner".
  It downloads one small file. Keep it somewhere safe (cloud drive, email to
  yourself). To restore: click "Restore backup" at the bottom and choose your file.
- Moving to a new computer or browser? Make a backup, open the planner there,
  and restore the backup.
- Best on a laptop or desktop. Opening local files on phones and tablets varies
  by device and browser - if you'd like to use it on a tablet, get in touch.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- MENU: on a laptop or desktop the pages are in a menu down the left side; on a phone or tablet they are in a row of tabs across the top.
- TODAY: your home dashboard, with a greeting, your intention, a year-progress ring, today's numbers, habit streaks, goal progress and your next key dates. A short "Getting started" checklist ticks itself off as you go (you can hide it). Plus Year, Month, Week and Day pages. Use the arrows to move to any year.
- PERSONALISE (the palette button at the top right): type your name for the greeting, pick a colour (Sage, Clay, Dusk or Rose), Light, Dark or Auto mode and a font. Your choice is saved with your planner.
- Want to see how a lived-in planner looks? Press "Look around with sample data" on the Today page. A banner lets you clear it and start fresh.
- Star a task to make it a KEY DATE: it is highlighted in the Year and Month pages.
- Habits, Goals, Reflect (monthly and yearly prompts) and Debt payoff: list what you owe, choose a snowball or avalanche plan, see your debt-free date and the interest you'd save, log payments, and use the built-in calculator with tip, sale, savings and loan shortcuts.
- NOTEBOOK (dark green button, top right): a separate bound book with coloured
  section tabs (Journal, Notes, Ideas - add your own with the + tab) and lined,
  blank, dot grid and squared paper.
    * Write: use the bar above the page for Title / Heading / Subhead, bold,
      italic, underline, lists, divider lines and links.
    * Decorate: add your own PHOTOS and choose from 64 STICKERS (nature, hearts,
      moon phases, tape, labels and more). Drag to move, use the round handles to
      resize and turn, and the bar to bring forward, send back, copy or delete.
    * Draw: switch to MARKUP to handwrite, highlight and sketch with a pen,
      marker or pencil using a mouse, finger or stylus.
  Your photos are included when you back up the planner.
